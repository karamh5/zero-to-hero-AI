## The whole pitch

I worked in compliance before this. And the thing about compliance is that it is not about knowing the rules, it is about proof.

Every enterprise is putting AI voice agents into legally governed conversations right now, and the launches are not stuck on the model. They are stuck because one person has to sign that the agent is safe and has nothing to base it on.

Today that proof is a person listening to somewhere between one and five percent of calls with a rulebook open next to them, weeks after go live.

[point at what was said]

EchoProof checks every turn before launch. This is a real thing an agent said, and it is illegal, because you cannot discuss somebody's debt with a person who is not them.

[point at the cream coloured card]

And here is the rule it broke, quoted exactly.

The important part is how. The model that made this call never got to browse the rulebook. It was handed one provision and had to answer from that text alone, and we print the provision next to the answer. So you are not trusting a verdict, you are checking one.

[scroll down to the evidence trace]

Underneath is every step, sealed so it breaks visibly if anyone edits it.

[stop scrolling and look at the room]

The closest thing that exists is OpenAI Presence, and it is end to end, so the vendor is grading itself. We are independent, and we work on cascaded stacks where the speech layer and the model come from different vendors.

The engine has nothing industry specific in it. Swap the rulebook and it runs telecom, or insurance.

What I want is an introduction to whoever signs off that an agent can go live.

## Questions you will get

### "How is it different from a guardrail product?"

Guardrails sit in the live call and have to answer in milliseconds, so they block. This runs before deployment where it can afford to think, and the output is a finding with a citation rather than a block.

### "What do you need from me?"

An introduction to whoever signs off that an agent can talk to customers, and ideally a live engagement to point it at.
