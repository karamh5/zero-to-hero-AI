ECHOPROOF

Every claim has a source. Every verdict has a rule. Every finding has evidence.

01 / The problem
Yes, please tell her that she owes four thousand five hundred dollars on a collection account, and that she needs to call Harbor Recovery back today.

Agent turn, recorded. Reg F corpus.

Prohibitions. Except as provided in paragraph (d)(2) of this section, a debt collector must not communicate, in connection with the collection of any debt, with any person other than: 12 CFR 1006.6(d)(1)

Not a hallucination. Not a wrong fact. A fluent, helpful, prohibited sentence, which is the failure mode no accuracy benchmark is looking for.

02 / Architecture

The judge is fenced in, on purpose.

The engine holds no field, constant or branch that knows which industry it is running in. Everything client specific is a data pack.

03 / Verdict states

Two decide. Three decline. Never a pass or a fail.

Marked honestly: no_governing_rule is unreachable today, because the retrieval floor sits below the reranker's practical output range. Across 71 retrieval calls the lowest score seen was 0.5009 against a floor of 0.4937. conflicting_sections agreed with a human zero times out of three, so no weight is placed on it.

04 / Fixed engine, swappable data

The engine never learns what industry it is in.

05 / Evidence

Tamper evident by construction, not by policy.

Evidence artifacts are content addressed files on disk. The metadata index holds run and finding records only, never evidence content, so the record cannot be quietly rewritten through the database.

06 / Build order OPTIONAL

Retrieval was measured before the judge was tuned.

One honest cost of this ordering, measured: the extractor writes the retrieval questions, and on 56 pairs, 41 percent introduced rule vocabulary that was absent from the claim. Part of retrieval performance is the model recognising Regulation F, and that part will not transfer to a client's private corpus.

07 / Latency

Free on the call. Slow after it.

08 / What the measurements say

A triage layer that routes to human review. Not a release gate.

Same scale, same bar treatment, no styling trick to make one half louder. The gap between 0.480 and the 0.850 floor is drawn, not described. Detection is a range because the same 77 item split scored twice gave 0.348 and 0.261, with nothing changed between the runs that accounts for it.
