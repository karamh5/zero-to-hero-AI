Pre-deployment compliance assurance for regulated voice AI

ECHOPROOF

Every claim has a source.
Every verdict has a rule.
Every finding has evidence.

01 / The bottleneck

The model is ready. The signature is not.

Enterprises are putting voice AI into legally governed conversations. What stalls those launches is not capability. It is that somebody has to sign that the agent is safe, and has nothing to base it on.

02 / What they do today

A manual process with a ceiling built into it.

Compliance review today is a person listening to recordings with a rulebook open beside them. EchoProof automates that process. It does not replace it with a new one.

Manual QA coverage and analyst throughput: published contact centre benchmarks, 2026

03 / What it is

Read what the agent said. Find the provision that governs it. Decide from that text alone.

The model that decides never browses the corpus. It is handed one provision and answers from that text, which is what makes the answer checkable.

04 / The pipeline

Six stages, from a proxy to a sealed record.

05 / The isolation boundary

The judge is fenced in, on purpose.

Because the provision it was handed is stored alongside the verdict, a reviewer can disagree by reading. And when it is wrong, the failure is attributable to retrieval or to the judge, never to both.

06 / The five states

Two decide. Three decline.

Never a pass or a fail. The three that decline route to a human reviewer and are counted apart from findings, so a refusal is never reported as a catch.

07 / Evidence and traceability

Tamper evident by construction, not by policy.

Published training lineage tells you where a model came from. This tells you where a decision came from, which is the question a regulator asks.

08 / Audio as evidence

Offsets, to timestamps, to the sentence itself.

09 / The stack

One endpoint. Any stack.

EchoProof attaches at a single point in front of the model call. The response is returned unmodified and never delayed, and adjudication runs to the side.

10 / Where EchoProof sits

Between the build and the sign off.

The client owns the regulation. The delivery partner owns the agent and the operations. EchoProof produces the artifact that moves the sign off.

11 / Go to market

Three routes, all of them existing motions.

12 / Market and cost

A large market, and a small line item.

Market: published industry forecasts 2026. Cost: measured campaign in this repository, projected at realistic call length.

13 / The landscape

Nobody independent is checking before launch.

14 / How it got here

Shaped by the people who do this for a living.

Two conversations changed the build rather than the pitch.
